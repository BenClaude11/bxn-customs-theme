We want you to be happy with your order. This policy explains your options if something isn't right. It doesn't limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

## Faulty or incorrect items

Our products come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. If an item arrives faulty or damaged, or isn't what you ordered, contact us and we'll arrange a repair, replacement or refund. For a major failure, you can choose a refund or a replacement. We cover the cost of returning faulty or incorrect items.

## Change of mind

We don't offer refunds or exchanges if you change your mind or order the wrong part, so please check fitment before you buy and contact us if you're unsure. If we told you a part would suit your bike and it doesn't, we'll treat that as our responsibility. This doesn't affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

## Refunds

Refunds are given for faulty, damaged or incorrect items as set out above. We cover the cost of shipping the item back to us. Approved refunds are paid to the bank account your payment came from.
