BXN Customs Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

BXN Customs (ABN 51 935 846 860) operates the BXN Customs online store and related online services.

This Privacy Policy explains how BXN Customs collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you visit our website, buy our products, create an account, contact us or otherwise interact with our services.

By using our website or giving us your personal information, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with BXN Customs, we may collect:

  • your name
  • your billing and shipping addresses
  • your email address
  • your phone number
  • account login details, where you create an account
  • products you view, add to your cart or buy
  • order and transaction details
  • payment-related information
  • your communications with us, including customer support enquiries
  • information you give us when asking about product compatibility or installation
  • device and browser information
  • your IP address and other technical information
  • how you use our website, including the pages you visit and your interactions with them
  • information you provide when you leave a product review or otherwise interact with our website.

We do not accept card payments, so we never receive your card details. When you pay by PayID or bank transfer, we see the payment details your bank provides, such as the account name, amount and reference.

2. How we collect personal information

We may collect personal information:

  • directly from you, when you place an order, create an account, contact us or otherwise give us information
  • automatically, when you use our website, including through cookies and similar technologies
  • through the service providers that host our website and send our order emails
  • from our bank, shipping providers and other businesses involved in processing your order
  • from third parties, where you have authorised them to share information with us or where the law permits it.

3. How we use your information

We may use your personal information to:

  • process and fulfil your orders
  • process payments and refunds
  • arrange shipping and delivery
  • provide customer support
  • answer compatibility and product questions
  • manage customer accounts
  • communicate with you about your orders
  • send you important information about products or services you have bought
  • improve our website, products and customer service
  • personalise your shopping experience
  • detect and prevent fraud, misuse and other harmful activity
  • keep our website and systems secure
  • keep appropriate business and transaction records
  • comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • resolve disputes and enforce our terms and policies
  • send marketing communications, where you have consented to receive them.

We only use personal information for purposes reasonably connected with our business, or as otherwise permitted or required by law.

4. Marketing communications

If you choose to receive marketing communications from BXN Customs, we may send you information about products, promotions, updates and other BXN Customs news.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or by contacting us. We will stop sending you marketing emails within 5 business days of your request.

Even if you opt out of marketing, we may still send you non-promotional messages about your orders, account, transactions, support requests or other necessary business matters.

5. Cookies and website technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to work correctly, remember your preferences, understand how the site is used and improve your shopping experience.

Our website stores your cart on your device (using your browser's local storage) so it is still there when you come back. Third-party services used on our website, such as our web host and font provider, may also use cookies or collect technical information.

You may be able to control or disable some cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how parts of the website work.

6. Website hosting and email services

Our website is hosted by a third-party web hosting provider. When you place an order or send us a message, the details you enter are sent to our hosting provider and to an email service so that the order or message reaches us, and so that we can send you your order confirmation and payment details.

These providers process personal information under their own privacy policies and may store it outside Australia.

7. Payments

Payments are made by PayID or bank transfer directly into our bank account. Your bank and ours process the payment under their own terms and privacy policies. We use the payment details we receive only to match payments to orders, process refunds and keep business records.

8. Shipping and fulfilment providers

To deliver your order, we may give relevant information to shipping companies, fulfilment providers, suppliers or other businesses involved in getting your order to you. Where reasonably necessary to fulfil your order, this may include your name, shipping address, phone number, email address and order details.

9. When we disclose personal information

We may disclose personal information to third parties where reasonably necessary to run our business, including:

  • website hosting and email service providers
  • our bank
  • shipping and delivery providers
  • website and technology providers
  • IT and cloud service providers
  • customer support providers
  • analytics providers
  • marketing and advertising providers, where applicable
  • professional advisers such as accountants, lawyers or insurers
  • government authorities or law enforcement, where required or permitted by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

We may also disclose information where you have asked or authorised us to, or where disclosure is otherwise permitted or required by law.

10. Third-party websites and services

Our website may link to third-party websites, including payment services, social media platforms and manufacturers' websites. If you follow a link to a third-party website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply.

BXN Customs is not responsible for the privacy, security or practices of websites we do not operate or control.

11. Social media

BXN Customs may use social media platforms and third-party integrations. If you interact with us through a social media platform, that platform may collect information about your interaction under its own privacy policy.

Anything you post publicly may be visible to other people.

12. Data security

We take reasonable steps, including technical and organisational measures, to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive or confidential information through unsecured channels unless it's necessary.

If a data breach involving your personal information is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the law requires it.

13. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. This may include keeping information to:

  • complete and keep records of transactions
  • provide customer support
  • manage accounts
  • meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations
  • resolve disputes
  • prevent fraud or misuse
  • enforce our agreements and policies.

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete it or securely dispose of it, subject to any legal requirement to keep it.

14. Accessing and correcting your information

You can contact us to ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or out of date.

We may need to verify your identity before processing some requests. We aim to respond within 30 days and will handle requests in line with applicable Australian privacy laws.

15. Deleting your information

You can contact us to ask us to delete personal information we hold about you. We will consider and respond to your request in line with applicable law.

We may be required to keep some information for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or other legitimate business purposes.

16. International disclosure and storage

Some of the businesses and service providers we use, including our website hosting and email providers, may store or process personal information outside Australia, including in the United States.

Where Australian privacy law requires it, BXN Customs will take reasonable steps to make sure the requirements for disclosing personal information overseas are met.

17. Children's privacy

Our website and services are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

18. Privacy complaints

If you have a concern or complaint about how BXN Customs has handled your personal information, please contact us first using the details below. We will investigate your complaint and respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

19. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our business, website, technology, privacy practices or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
