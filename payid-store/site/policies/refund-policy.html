BXN Customs Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Refund Policy explains how returns, refunds, exchanges and cancellations work at BXN Customs (ABN 51 935 846 860). It forms part of our Terms of Service.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies those rights.

1. Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If an item is faulty, arrives damaged, is not as described, or is not what you ordered, you are entitled to a remedy under the Australian Consumer Law.

  • Major failure: you can choose a refund or a replacement.
  • Minor failure: we will repair, replace or refund the item.

Please contact us as soon as you notice a problem. If an item arrives damaged, please let us know within 7 days of delivery where possible, with photos of the item and its packaging, so we can lodge a claim with the carrier. This request does not limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

For faulty, damaged or incorrect items, we will cover the reasonable cost of returning the item to us, and we will send you a prepaid return label where possible.

Damage caused by installation that does not follow our instructions, misuse, modification or any of the other matters listed in section 9 of our Terms of Service is not a fault with the product.

2. Change of mind

We accept change-of-mind returns within 14 days of delivery, as long as the item is:

  • unused, not installed and never connected to a bike, battery or electrical system
  • in its original condition and packaging, with all parts, connectors and accessories included

For change-of-mind returns:

  • you pay the cost of returning the item, and we recommend using a tracked service
  • original shipping costs are not refunded
  • we refund the price of the item once we have received and inspected it

Electrical parts that have been installed or connected cannot be returned for change of mind, because we can't confirm they are undamaged and safe to sell again.

3. Ordered the wrong part, or it doesn't fit

If a product doesn't suit your bike, and it is not faulty and matches its description, the return is treated as a change-of-mind return under section 2.

If we told you before you ordered that the product would suit your bike or setup and it doesn't, we will treat it as our responsibility: you can choose a refund or an exchange, and we will cover return shipping.

4. How to start a return

Email us at benw98136@gmail.com before sending anything back, with:

  • your order number
  • the item or items you want to return
  • the reason for the return
  • photos or a short video if the item is faulty or damaged

We will reply with return instructions and the return address. Items sent back without contacting us first may take longer to identify and process.

Please pack items securely. We can't refund change-of-mind returns that are damaged in transit back to us because of poor packaging.

5. Inspection and refunds

When your return arrives, we will inspect it and email you to confirm the outcome.

Approved refunds are paid by bank transfer or PayID to the account your payment came from, within 5 business days of approval. For your security, we only refund to the original paying account.

If a change-of-mind return doesn't meet the conditions in section 2, we will contact you and can send the item back to you at your cost.

6. Exchanges

If you want a different item, the quickest way is to return the original under this policy and place a new order. If an item is faulty, we will replace it with the same item where it is in stock, or refund you if it isn't.

7. Cancelling an order

If you want to cancel an order, email us as soon as possible. If your order hasn't been dispatched yet, we will cancel it and refund you in full.

Once an order has been dispatched it can't be cancelled, but you can return it under this policy.
