BXN Customs Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how BXN Customs (ABN 51 935 846 860) processes and delivers orders. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Where we ship

We currently ship within Australia only.

2. Processing and dispatch

Orders are processed Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Payment is by PayID or bank transfer, and we dispatch once your payment has arrived. In-stock items are usually dispatched within 1 to 3 business days of your payment being received. Orders that are not paid within 72 hours may be cancelled.

If an item is delayed or out of stock, we will contact you. You can choose to wait for it or cancel that item for a full refund.

3. Shipping methods and costs

Orders are sent with Australia Post, with tracking. Shipping is a flat $11.95 anywhere in Australia, and free on orders of $150 or more. The shipping cost is shown at checkout before you place your order.

4. Delivery times

Delivery times are estimates from the date of dispatch and depend on your location. Most orders arrive within 2 to 8 business days of dispatch.

Regional and remote areas may take longer. Delivery times are not guaranteed, and we are not responsible for delays caused by carriers, weather, natural disasters or other events outside our reasonable control.

5. Tracking

When your order is dispatched, we will email you a tracking number so you can follow your parcel.

6. Your delivery address

Please check your delivery address carefully at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us straight away. We can update the address if the order hasn't been dispatched yet.

If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incorrect or incomplete, or because it wasn't collected from a post office or parcel locker, we will contact you. We can send it again once you pay the new shipping cost, or refund the order minus the original shipping cost.

We can deliver to PO Boxes and Parcel Lockers when your order is sent with Australia Post.

7. Safe drop and signatures

The carrier may leave your parcel in a safe place at your address. Once the carrier records it as delivered to your address, it is treated as delivered. If you would prefer a signature on delivery, email us before your order is dispatched.

8. Lost or delayed parcels

If your tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, or says delivered but you can't find your parcel, please check around your property and with neighbours, then email us with your order number.

We will lodge an investigation with the carrier and keep you updated. If the carrier confirms your parcel has been lost, we will send a replacement or give you a full refund.

9. Damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, please take photos of the packaging and the item before throwing anything away, and email us, ideally within 7 days of delivery. Damaged items are handled under our Refund Policy.

10. Batteries and dangerous goods

Some items, such as lithium batteries, are classed as dangerous goods for transport. They can only be sent by road with certain shipping methods, may take longer to arrive, and may not be available for delivery to some locations.
